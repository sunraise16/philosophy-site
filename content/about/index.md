This site is a personal space for thinking through philosophy, not as a finished system, but as an ongoing practice of reading, questioning, and writing.

Here is what each section contains:

## Home
An introduction to the site itself. Here you will find a brief overview of what Meilong Philosophy is, who it is for, and how to navigate the content.

## Reading Notes
Book recommendations, personal reflections, and reviews of philosophical works, from classical texts to contemporary writings. Each note is an attempt to engage with a book, not just summarize it.

## Concept Development
A look at how key philosophical concepts have evolved throughout the history of Western philosophy. This section traces the development of ideas such as being, knowledge, justice, freedom, and truth, from ancient Greece to modern thought, showing how meanings shift across thinkers and eras.

## Articles
Original personal essays. These are my own writings, reflections, arguments, and explorations that draw on philosophical traditions but aim to speak in my own voice.

Whether you are here to discover a new book, trace an idea through history, or read a personal essay, I hope you find something worth thinking about.
